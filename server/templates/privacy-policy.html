2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We receive only a purchase token from Google to verify your subscription server-side. We do not see or store any payment card or bank details.   • Android Version: Collected to determine the correct storage method (Legacy / Scoped / SAF) for your device.
  • Subscription Status: Stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud, India region) to validate your Pro membership status across devices and sessions.
  • AdMob Analytics: Google AdMob may collect anonymised analytics and ad-interaction data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. This applies only to free-tier users seeing ads.
  • Crash Reports: If the app crashes, anonymised crash data may be sent to Expo/EAS to help us fix bugs. No personal data is included.

2.3 What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not access, read, upload, or transmit any WhatsApp statuses, images, or videos to any server.
  • We do not read your WhatsApp messages or contacts.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for targeted advertising (beyond what Google AdMob does autonomously on the free tier).

4. Data Storage and Security

Subscription data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore, which is hosted on Google Cloud infrastructure with encryption at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL). Payment order records are retained for up to 7 years as required by Indian tax law (GST compliance).

We implement the following security measures:

  • HMAC-SHA256 signature verification on all Razorpay payments (Razorpay builds)
  • Server-side Google Play purchase token verification via the Google Play Developer API (Play Store build)
  • Firebase Authentication token verification on every authenticated API request
  • No raw payment credentials stored on our servers
  • All API communication over HTTPS

6. Storage Permissions

StatusVault uses the Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) to obtain read access ONLY to the WhatsApp Media folder you explicitly select. We never access any other files on your device. All file operations happen locally — no files are ever transmitted to any server.

Permissions actually declared by the app:

  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — required to save statuses to your gallery on Android 9 or below; ignored on Android 10+ (the app uses scoped MediaStore APIs instead).
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — Google Play Billing in-app purchases (Play Store build only).
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — optional, only when you opt in to status-reminder notifications (Android 13+).
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — re-arms scheduled reminder notifications after a phone reboot.
  • VIBRATE — short haptic taps on a few buttons.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — required by Google AdMob (free tier only).

Permissions explicitly BLOCKED at the manifest level (so they cannot be granted even by accident):

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
  • READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
  • READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED
  • RECORD_AUDIO
  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW

Because the broad READ_MEDIA_* permissions are blocked, StatusVault is structurally incapable of reading your full photo or video library — even if a future update tried to. The only media access available to the app is whatever folder you choose in the SAF folder picker.

7. Referral & Rewards Data

StatusVault offers two distinct ways to earn free Pro access without payment:

7.1 Personal Invite & Earn (Reward Ladder)

Every signed-in user is automatically issued a unique 6-character invite code. We store, against your Firebase UID, the code itself, the count and Firebase UIDs of users you have referred, the timestamp at which they joined, the reward tiers you have already claimed, and the resulting Pro expiry date. The Reward Ladder grants:

  • 3 friends → 48 hours of Pro
  • 10 friends → 1 week of Pro
  • 50 friends → 1 month of Pro
  • 100 friends → 3 months of Pro
  • 500 friends → 1.5 years of Pro (548 days)

Earned days STACK on top of any existing Pro time — you never lose paid days. To prevent abuse, one device fingerprint can only be the source of a single attribution and a user can have only one referrer (immutable once set).

7.2 Influencer / Giveaway Codes

Limited-quantity codes shared by partners can be redeemed once per user from the Subscription screen. We store the code you redeemed, the device fingerprint that claimed it, and the resulting Pro expiry date. Codes cannot be redeemed if you already have an active Pro subscription, and one device fingerprint can only redeem a given code once.

Both systems run inside our backend on Firebase Firestore; no third party sees this data.

8. Refund Policy

8.1 Google Play Store Purchases

Refunds follow Google Play's standard policy. You can request a refund directly within the Play Store app within 48 hours of purchase: Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Order history → your StatusVault order → Request a refund. For issues beyond 48 hours, email us with your Google Order ID.

8.2 Indus App Store / Other Store Purchases (Razorpay)

We will issue a refund in any of the following cases:

  • You were charged but Pro was not activated due to a verified technical failure (contact us within 7 days with your Razorpay Payment ID).
  • You were charged twice for the same period.
  • The app is fundamentally non-functional on your device and we are unable to resolve it within 14 days of your report.

Refund window by plan:

  • 1 Month (₹29): Refund only in the cases listed above. Change-of-mind refunds are not issued for this plan because the transaction value barely exceeds the payment-gateway fee.
  • 3 Months (₹79) / 1 Year (₹149): Full refund within 48 hours of purchase if the request meets the criteria above.

Email ahmedsameerbinan2@gmail.com with your Razorpay Payment ID and a description of the issue. Decisions are made within 5 business days; approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.

Anti-abuse: users who initiate a bank chargeback in lieu of using the refund process above will be permanently banned from future purchases.
