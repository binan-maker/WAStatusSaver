💡 Pro Tips While Waiting
AdMob Approval Timeline: Usually 2-4 weeks. If >6 weeks, contact AdMob support with your app package name + Play Store draft link.
Revenue Strategy: Focus on Razorpay + Google Play subscriptions first. Many utility apps earn 60-80% of revenue from payments before ads stabilize.
Test Device IDs: Add your physical device to testDeviceIds to see test ads on release builds safely.
Frequency Capping: Even with test ads, implement INTERSTITIAL_COOLDOWN_MS and VIDEO_AD_FREQUENCY so UX feels polished when live.
🎯 Final Architecture Note
Your app is already structured correctly for this phase:
✅ Ad hooks are isolated (hooks/ads/)
✅ Payments are decoupled from ads
✅ Fallback handling is policy-safe
✅ Environment switching is trivial
You don't need to wait for AdMob to ship. Use test mode, verify payments, optimize placements, and flip the config when approved. This is exactly how Instagram, TikTok, and 90% of successful utility apps handle the approval gap.
