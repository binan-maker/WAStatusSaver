To maximize revenue from a small user base (especially in high-value markets like the US and high-volume markets like India), you need a "High-Frequency, High-Engagement" ad strategy.
Here is the professional analysis of where and how to place every ad type for maximum profit:
1. The "Gold Mine": App Open Ads
Placement: Triggered exactly when the user opens the app or returns to the app after being in WhatsApp.
Why it makes money: This is the first thing a user sees. It has a very high eCPM (cost per thousand impressions) because it captures 100% of the user's attention before they even start using the app.
Strategy: Every time a user switches from WhatsApp back to your app to save a status, they should see this.
2. The Home Page (Images & Videos Grid)
Ad Type: Native Advanced (High Priority)
Placement: Inserted directly into the grid (e.g., every 6th or 9th item).
Why it makes money: Because it looks like a status card, users don't "ignore" it like they do with banners. It gets more clicks and higher revenue.
Ad Type: Anchored Adaptive Banner
Placement: Fixed at the very bottom of the screen.
Why it makes money: It stays on screen the entire time the user is scrolling. It refreshes every 30-60 seconds, generating multiple impressions from a single user session.
3. The Full-Screen Viewer (Inside Images/Videos)
Ad Type: Interstitial (The "Big Earner")
Placement: Triggered when a user clicks a status to view it in full screen, or when they click the "Save" button.
Why it makes money: Interstitials have the highest payout of all standard ads.
Strategy: Do not show it on every click (that kills the app). Show it every 3rd or 4th status view. This keeps the user happy but keeps the money flowing.
Ad Type: Rewarded Interstitial (Beta)
Placement: "Watch a quick ad to unlock 'Batch Save' (saving all statuses at once)."
Why it makes money: Users hate saving 20 statuses one by one. They will gladly watch a 15-second ad to save them all at once.
4. The "Saved" Page
Ad Type: Native Advanced
Placement: A large native ad at the top of the "Saved" list.
Why it makes money: Users often come here to "re-watch" or "re-share" content. A native ad here feels premium and doesn't interrupt the "re-watching" experience.
5. The Settings Page
Ad Type: Banner or Small Native
Placement: At the very bottom of the settings list.
Why it makes money: Settings is a "low-traffic" page, but it’s a good place to put a constant banner. It’s "free money" from a page that usually has no ads.
🌍 Global Strategy: India vs. International (US/UK)
For India (High Volume, Low Payout):
Focus on Native Ads and Banners. Since the payout per ad is lower in India, you need "Volume." By placing Native ads in the grid, you ensure that every scroll generates money.
For US/International (Low Volume, High Payout):
Focus on App Open and Interstitials. One user in the US seeing an Interstitial ad can make you as much money as 50 users in India seeing a Banner. You want to make sure these high-value users see the "Big Ads" (Full-screen) at least twice per session.
💰 Summary of the "Ultimate Money" Setup:
App Open: Every time the app starts.
Native Advanced: Every 6 items in the Home Grid.
Interstitial: Every 3rd "View" or "Save" action.
Rewarded: To unlock "HD Download" or "Remove Ads for 24 Hours."
Adaptive Banner: Always visible at the bottom of every screen.
Final Tip: The "Rewarded" ad is your secret weapon. If you tell a user "Watch a video to remove all other ads for today," they will do it. You get a high-payout Rewarded ad view, and the user gets a clean app for the rest of the day. Both win.
