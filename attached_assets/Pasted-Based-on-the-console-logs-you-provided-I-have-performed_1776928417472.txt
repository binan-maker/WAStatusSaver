Based on the console logs you provided, I have performed a technical analysis of exactly why your app is experiencing delays, freezes, and infinite log cycles.
Here is the explanation of the "Anatomy of the Freeze" on your device:
1. The 4.6 Second "Total Blackout" (Video Delay)
The logs show exactly why videos feel "stuck" when you click them. It is a two-step hardware bottleneck:
Step 1 (File Copy): LOG [Media] Copy complete for ... (2035ms). It takes 2 full seconds just to copy the video from WhatsApp's protected folder to the app's cache.
Step 2 (Engine Load): LOG [Viewer] replaceAsync complete ... (2642ms). The video engine takes another 2.6 seconds to process that file.
The Result: You click a video and nothing happens for nearly 5 seconds. During this time, the phone's memory is 100% busy, which is why the "Back" button feels like it's not working—the phone isn't "listening" to your fingers while it's copying.
2. The "Infinite Throttled" Loop
The reason you see [Loader] AppState active, but throttled repeating endlessly is a "Focus Tug-of-War."
The Culprit: The AdMob system. Every time you see ERROR App open ad error: [googleMobileAds/no-fill], the Ad SDK is trying to show a window, failing, and closing it.
The Loop:
The Ad fails and closes.
Android says: "The app is now Active again!"
The App says: "I see I'm active, I should Refresh the status list!"
The App says: "Wait, I just refreshed 1 second ago, so I will Throttle (skip) the refresh."
Steps 1–4 repeat every few milliseconds, creating the infinite loop.
3. Touch Event "Dropping" (The Freeze)
You see warnings like Cannot record touch end without a touch start.
Because the app is rapidly flickering between "Active" and "Background" (due to the Ad errors and focus shifts), the Android system gets confused.
You touch the screen while it's active, but before you lift your finger, the app blinks into the background for a millisecond. Android then deletes your touch, so the "Click" never finishes. This is why you feel you have to click 5 times before it opens.
4. Background Permission "Static"
The log Missing MEDIA_LIBRARY permissions is constantly firing in the background.
Even though you are looking at statuses, the app is trying to scan your personal gallery every time the app wakes up.
Because permission is missing, it crashes that specific background task. This adds "noise" to the system, slowing down the already-slow video copying process.
5. UI Rendering Pressure
The log VirtualizedList: You have a large list that is slow to update tells us that the Home Page is trying to render all 30 thumbnails at once while the app is already struggling with the Ad-Loop and the Video Copying.
Summary
Your app isn't "broken" in the sense of having bad logic—it is fighting the phone's hardware.
Android 11+ SAF is making the file copy too slow (2 seconds).
AdMob No-Fill is making the app "blink," which clears your touch events.
