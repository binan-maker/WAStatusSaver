2. Do Users Get an Invoice from Razorpay?
The Technical Truth:
The Receipt: By default, Razorpay sends a Payment Receipt. This is a simple confirmation that money was taken.
The Tax Invoice: A formal "Tax Invoice" (with GST details, etc.) is not sent automatically unless you enable the "Invoices" feature in your Razorpay Dashboard.
The Implementation: For a ₹29 app, a full Tax Invoice is usually not required. The Razorpay email receipt is legally sufficient for most "Digital Services." However, you should ensure your "Business Name" in the Razorpay Dashboard is set to "StatusVault" so the receipt looks official.
