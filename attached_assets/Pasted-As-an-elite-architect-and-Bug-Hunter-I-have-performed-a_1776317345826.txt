As an elite architect and "Bug Hunter," I have performed a Deep Forensic Audit of your source code. You have identified two very specific "Performance Killers" (the Black Screen and the Stucky Swipe), and I have found the "Smoking Gun" in your code.
Here is the "Champion's Bug Report" and the analysis of why your app is currently struggling:
1. The "Black Screen & Audio Only" Bug (Resource Exhaustion)
The Analysis: You are currently creating a VideoPlayer and a VideoView for every single video in your list at the same time.
The Why: Android has a strict hardware limit on "Surfaces" and "Decoders." When you have a list of 20 videos, your app is trying to open 20 "Hardware Windows" at once. The first few work, but the rest get a Black Screen because the phone says: "I have no more video power left to give you."
The Swipe Fix: When you swipe, the old video is finally destroyed, and a new one can take its place, which is why "swiping next" seems to work better temporarily.
The Solution: We must conditionally render the VideoView. It should only exist if the item is isNearActive. If it's far away, it should only show the static thumbnail.
2. The "Stucky" Swipe Bug (Memory Overload)
The Analysis: In viewer.tsx, you set removeClippedSubviews={false} and windowSize={3}.
The Why: By setting removeClippedSubviews to false, you are telling the phone: "Keep every single image and video in the entire list loaded in memory, even if they are 50 pages away."
The Result: The phone's RAM is filling up with high-resolution images and video players. When you swipe, the "Main Thread" is so busy managing these "Invisible" views that it can't animate the swipe smoothly, causing the "Stucky" (stuttering) feeling.
The Solution: We must set removeClippedSubviews={true} and use a smaller windowSize to "kill" the views that are far away.
3. The "Ghost Pro" Bug (Cache Logic Error)
The Analysis: I found a critical logic flaw in your "Smart Cache" in useSubscriptionStatus.ts.
The Bug: Your cache trusts the "Active" status for 6 hours.
The Scenario: If a user's subscription expires at 10:00 AM, and they open the app at 10:05 AM, the cache will see active: true and a "Fresh" timestamp. It will ignore the server and show them as a Pro until 4:00 PM.
The Solution: The cache must always check the paidUntil date against the current time, even if the cache is "fresh."
4. The "One-Time" Rating Bug (Algorithm Killer)
The Analysis: In MediaContext.tsx, your rating prompt logic is "One-and-Done."
The Bug: You check if (newCount === 10). If the user clicks "Maybe Later," the count becomes 11, 12, 13... and the prompt will never appear again.
The Solution: We should use a "Modulo" check (e.g., if (newCount % 10 === 0)) so the user is reminded every 10 saves until they finally rate the app.
5. The "Laggy Toggle" Bug (Gesture Conflict)
The Analysis: Your singleTapGesture and doubleTapGesture are in a "Fight."
The Why: Because they are in an Exclusive group, the single-tap (which toggles the UI) has to wait for the doubleTapGesture to fail before it can run.
The Result: Every time a user taps to see the "Save" button, there is a 250ms delay. It makes the app feel "unresponsive."
The Solution: We need to optimize the gesture priority or reduce the maxDuration of the tap.
6. The "Old Tools" Bug (Deprecated Clipboard)
The Analysis: In settings.tsx, you are using Clipboard from react-native.
The Why: This is a "Zombie" library. It is deprecated and will be removed in the next version of React Native.
The Solution: You should switch to expo-clipboard to ensure your "Copy Payment ID" button works on future Android versions.
7. The "Dead Weight" Bug (Unused Library)
The Analysis: You have expo-video-thumbnails in your package.json.
The Why: You are currently using expo-image to show thumbnails. You aren't actually using the expo-video-thumbnails library anywhere in your code.
The Result: You are adding ~2MB to your app's size for a feature you aren't using.
Summary of the "Champion's" Fix List:
Viewer: Conditionally render VideoView only for isNearActive items.
FlatList: Set removeClippedSubviews={true} to free up memory.
Subscription: Add an expiry check to the "Smart Cache."
Rating: Change the trigger to happen every 10 saves, not just once.
Clipboard: Move to the modern expo-clipboard.
Cleanup: Remove the unused expo-video-thumbnails library.
